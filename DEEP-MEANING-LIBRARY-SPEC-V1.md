# MEOW TAROT V3.6 — 78-Card Deep Meaning Library

## Goal
Replace generic Minor Arcana `Suit + Rank` composition with card-specific meanings for all 56 Minor Arcana while preserving the 22 Major Arcana contextual library.

## Coverage
- 22 Major Arcana: contextual meanings retained for LOVE / CAREER / MONEY / DECISION.
- 56 Minor Arcana: individually authored contextual meanings for LOVE / CAREER / MONEY / DECISION / OTHER.
- Each Minor also has a card-specific reversed nuance.
- Total contextual library covers all 78 cards.

## Why this matters
`Eight of Swords` is no longer treated as a generic Eight + Swords card. It now specifically represents constraint, fear and narrowed options. `Seven of Cups` now focuses on options/fantasy/uncertainty, `Five of Pentacles` on lack/support, `Ten of Wands` on burden, etc.

## Combination integration
V3.6 also gives every Minor Arcana card semantic tags used by Combination Intelligence. Strong transition/tension rules are evaluated before generic shared-theme support, and generic reversal tags are excluded from support matching.

## Reality boundary
Card meanings are symbolic interpretations, not proof of hidden facts. In particular, Seven of Swords can indicate avoidance, incomplete information or strategy, but does not prove infidelity or deception by itself.
