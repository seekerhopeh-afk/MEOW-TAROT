# MEOW TAROT V3.7 — Intent × Card Precision Engine

## Goal
A card is no longer interpreted only from broad domains such as LOVE, CAREER, MONEY, or DECISION. V3.7 applies a second lens from the user's exact intent.

Examples:
- Two of Cups + FEELINGS → mutual response / visible connection signals.
- Two of Cups + RETURN → whether reconnection can become reciprocal.
- Two of Cups + THIRD_PARTY → relationship clarity; never proof of a third party.
- Death + JOB_CHANGE → old work cycle nearing completion / transition.
- Death + RETURN → old relationship form is ending; returning requires a changed pattern.

## Flow
QUESTION → EXACT INTENT → DOMAIN MEANING → INTENT PRECISION → POSITION → CARD RELATIONSHIP → COMBINATION → ANSWER.

## Intent profiles
The engine has precision profiles for THIRD_PARTY, FEELINGS, RETURN, RELATIONSHIP_FUTURE, JOB_CHANGE, RESIGN, PROMOTION, JOB_OUTCOME, CAREER_CURRENT, WORK_RELATIONSHIP, BUSINESS_OUTCOME, MONEY_RETURN, MONEY_CURRENT, DEBT, INVESTMENT, DECISION, and TIMING.

Each profile defines intent-specific supportive and caution semantic tags. Every card is scored through that profile, with extra weight on card 3 because it carries direction/outcome.

## Card-specific overrides
Important cards with meanings that materially change by intent have explicit override text, including Two of Cups, Six of Cups, Eight of Cups, Three of Swords, Seven of Swords, Ace of Pentacles, The Hermit, The Moon, Justice, Death, and The Chariot.

## Safety boundary
THIRD_PARTY never converts secrecy, Moon, Seven of Swords, Three of Swords, or any combination into factual proof of infidelity. FEELINGS remains a reading of relationship signals and behavior, not private-thought certainty. INVESTMENT remains reflective and not buy/sell advice.
